# Content provenance and licensing

This repository contains prompts, scripts, templates, and/or generated-content instructions. Before distributing any asset commercially, verify:

- who authored the source material;
- the terms of the generation platform used;
- whether referenced names, voices, images, or trademarks require permission;
- whether the intended sales channel permits the output format.

Do not represent generated content as human-authored when disclosure is required. Keep a provenance note with releases and avoid committing private customer material.

## Release checklist

- Confirm every shipped asset is listed in the release manifest.
- Confirm licenses and attribution are included where required.
- Confirm no secrets or personal data are present.
- Confirm links in the README resolve to files in this repository.
